HealthSync — Your Personal Health & Fitness Management System

Record health data, track workouts, reach goals and earn rewards. Everything is connected: finish a workout and your progress, reports, challenges and points update together.

♡Health MonitoringWeight, heart rate and blood pressure records.
◈Fitness ManagementPlan routines that fit your day.
⚡Workout TrackingEnter values, mark workouts done.
◔Progress TrackingDaily, weekly and monthly progress.
◎GoalsRealistic weight and activity goals.
★Challenges & RewardsEarn points and keep your streak.
▤Medical Report ManagementKeep a list of your report details.

HealthSync is a student project prototype. Data is stored locally in the browser and should not be used for storing real sensitive medical records.
